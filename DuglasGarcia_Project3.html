What makes a song dominate Spotify Wrapped? This project explores the top 50 most-streamed songs of 2025 — who made them, what genres they belong to, and what audio features they share. Then it lets you build your own.

The artists of 2025

The top 5 songs of the year tell the story of a few breakout artists dominating the charts. Sabrina Carpenter alone claimed three of the five spots — a feat almost unheard of in a single Wrapped cycle.

What genres ruled the charts?

Pop remains the undisputed king — nearly a third of all top 50 songs fall under the genre. But Indie Pop, Hip-Hop, and Country each carved out meaningful space, reflecting a more fragmented streaming landscape than ever before.
